Delete Your Account

You can delete your Sharp Boy account and everything attached to it at any time. This page explains how, what is removed, what is kept and how long it takes.

Applies to Sharp Boy · Bundle ID com.zyraflow.sharpboy · Last updated 15 November 2026

Delete it inside the app

  1. Open Sharp Boy and sign in to the account you want to remove.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account & Privacy.
  3. Choose Delete Account and type DELETE to confirm.
  4. The app signs you out and your account is queued for removal immediately.

Delete it by email

If you cannot sign in, write to support@thinkzet.com from the email address on the account, with the subject Sharp Boy account deletion request. We reply within 7 days to confirm we received it and complete the deletion within 30 days.

We may ask you to confirm you control the account before deleting it. This protects you from someone else deleting your data.

What is deleted

ItemWhat happensTiming
Account record (email, display name)Deleted from the production databaseImmediately
Content you created in the appDeleted from the production databaseImmediately
Sign-in sessions and tokensRevokedImmediately
Backup copiesAge out on the normal backup cycleWithin 30 days
Crash and diagnostic logsDeleted, and not linked to you in any caseWithin 90 days

What is kept, and why

  • Purchase and tax records. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own records of a transaction under their policies and tax law. We cannot delete those on your behalf.
  • Aggregated counts. A number such as "one account was deleted today" may remain, with nothing that identifies you.
  • Records we must keep by law. Retained only for as long as the law requires, and used for nothing else.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription

A subscription is billed by Apple or Google, not by us, so deleting the account leaves it running. Cancel it first, or you will keep being charged. The steps are on the subscriptions page.
