Subscriptions and Billing

Sharp Boy Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store and Google Play. This page explains what you get, what it costs, how it renews and how to cancel it.

Applies to Sharp Boy · Bundle ID com.zyraflow.sharpboy · Last updated 15 November 2026

The price you pay is the one displayed on the purchase sheet at the moment you confirm. It is set per country by the App Store or Google Play and includes the tax that applies where you live. The same two plans belong to the subscription group Sharp Boy Premium, so you can move between them without losing access.

What Premium unlocks

Premium is a single entitlement. When it is active, every paid feature in the app is available and nothing is metered. When it lapses, the app keeps working on the free tier and your data stays where it is.

Renewal

  • Payment is charged to your Apple or Google account at confirmation of purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically for the same period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can see the next renewal date in the app under Settings > Subscription, and in your store account.

Cancel

On iOS

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Sharp Boy, then Cancel Subscription.

On Android

  1. Open Google Play and tap your profile.
  2. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Sharp Boy, then Cancel subscription.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Access continues until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app, or deleting your account, does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store that took the payment, under its own policy. We cannot issue a refund for an Apple or Google purchase directly.

  • Apple: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Google Play: request one at Google Play refunds.

If a refund is granted, the Premium entitlement ends when the store confirms it.

Restore a purchase

If you reinstall the app or move to a new device, open the paywall and choose Restore Purchases. The entitlement is restored from your store account. If it does not appear, check that you are signed in to the store with the same account you bought it on.

Price changes

If the price of a plan changes, the store notifies you before the change applies to your renewal, and you can cancel before it takes effect. We never charge a new price without that notice.
