Each category in detail

Email address

Collected when you create an account or sign in, so you can recover your data on a new device. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with a third party other than the processors named in the privacy policy. You can use the app without an account, in which case no email is collected.

Name or display name

Collected only if you type one. It is shown back to you inside the app and is not published anywhere.

Content you create in the app

Whatever you enter, record or upload is stored so the app can show it to you again and sync it across your devices. It is not read by a human, not used to train a model, and not shared. Deleting the item in the app, or deleting your account, removes it.

Purchase history

We receive whether your Premium entitlement is active, which plan you bought, and the renewal or expiry date. The receipt is validated by Apple or Google through RevenueCat. We never receive your card number, billing address or the amount your bank charged.

Crash and diagnostic data

Collected when the app fails, so the fault can be fixed. It contains the app version, operating system version, device model and a stack trace. It is not linked to your account and is deleted after 90 days.

What is never collected

  • Your precise or coarse location.
  • Your contacts, calendar, photo library, microphone or camera, unless you deliberately attach something.
  • Health data from Apple Health, HealthKit or Google Fit.
  • Your advertising identifier, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • Your payment card, bank account or billing address.

Your controls

  1. Use it without an account. No email, no display name, no sync.
  2. Turn off diagnostics. In the app settings, or in iOS under Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, and on Android under Settings > Google > Usage & diagnostics.
  3. Delete an item. Remove it inside the app and it is removed from our database.
  4. Delete everything. Follow the account deletion page.
