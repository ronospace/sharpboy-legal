Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Sharp Boy collects, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept and how you get rid of it. It applies to the Sharp Boy iOS and Android apps and to any purchase made inside them.

Applies to Sharp Boy · Bundle ID com.zyraflow.sharpboy · Operated by ZyraFlow GmbH · Last updated 15 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Sharp Boy is published by ZyraFlow GmbH ("we", "us"), which is the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us at support@thinkzet.com for any privacy question, request or complaint.

2. What we collect

We collect three kinds of information, and nothing beyond what the app needs to work.

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account identifier. If you create an account, we store your email address and a display name so you can sign in on a new device and recover your data.
  • Content you enter. Anything you type, record or upload inside the app is stored so the app can show it back to you.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we keep the message and our reply so we can resolve the issue.

2.2 Information created by using the app

  • Subscription state. Whether your Premium entitlement is active, which plan you bought, and when it renews or expires. This is read from Apple or Google through RevenueCat. We never see your card number.
  • Technical logs. App version, operating system version, device model, language, and error reports with a timestamp. These are used to fix crashes and are not used to build an advertising profile.

2.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, your photo library, your health records from Apple Health or Google Fit, your advertising identifier for cross-app tracking, or your payment card details. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.

5. Where it is stored and how it is protected

Data is stored on servers in the European Union and, where a processor is based elsewhere, transferred under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. All traffic between the app and our servers uses TLS. Passwords, where used, are stored only as salted hashes. Access to production data is limited to the people who need it to run the service.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and content: until you delete your account, then removed as described in section 7.
  • Support messages: 24 months after the conversation closes.
  • Technical logs: 90 days, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Purchase records: retained by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own policies, and by us for as long as tax law requires.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete your account inside the app, or by following the steps on the account deletion page. Deletion removes your account record and the content attached to it from our production database, and a backup copy ages out within 30 days. It does not cancel a subscription - see subscriptions for that.

8. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or a jurisdiction with similar law, you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Write to support@thinkzet.com and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local supervisory authority.

California residents have the right to know, delete, correct and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA.

9. Children

Sharp Boy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data we will update this page and change the date at the top. A change that materially affects your rights will be announced in the app before it takes effect.
